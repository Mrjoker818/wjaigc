# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Designers, visual creators, and internal operators who need to generate images, refine prompts, inspect layers, manage assets, and keep projects organized across sessions.

## Product Purpose

WujieAI is an AI creation workspace for image generation, prompt refinement, infinite canvas editing, PSD export, and project management. It should help designers move from idea to editable output quickly, with strong control over assets, models, and exported files.

## Brand Personality

Confident, precise, capable.

The experience should feel like a serious creative tool, not a demo. It should be calm under pressure, efficient for repeated use, and trustworthy when handling projects, models, and exports.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-app styling, overly decorative SaaS gradients, playful consumer onboarding, cluttered card walls, modal-heavy flows, and anything that feels like a template dashboard. Do not make the product feel toy-like or promotional.

## Design Principles

- Make the core task obvious immediately.
- Keep creation, editing, and export flows connected.
- Prefer control, traceability, and recoverability over surprise.
- Reveal complexity only when it becomes useful.
- Keep the interface dense but legible for repeat use.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, readable contrast, clear labels, useful error states, and reduced-motion friendliness. Keep touch targets usable on smaller screens and avoid relying on color alone for state.
